Scoliosis Surgery Bleeding & Transfusion Risk

A literature-weighted screening worksheet for preoperative risk assessment.

Purpose and Honest Scope

This is a transparent, literature-weighted screening worksheet for preoperative bleeding/transfusion risk in scoliosis surgery. It is not a prospectively validated probability calculator. Points are assigned in proportion to published odds ratios (log-odds informed) so the tally tracks known risk, but the absolute point cutoffs are pragmatic and should be validated locally before any hard clinical rule is applied. Blood-Conservation Measures

Measures with the strongest evidence in this population: tranexamic acid (Level I RCT: 27% reduction in intraoperative blood loss and fewer transfusions in AIS), preoperative anemia/iron correction, cell salvage, acute normovolemic hemodilution, normothermia, and shortening operative time.

Important Caveats

  • Point weights are log-odds informed, not prospectively derived; treat the total as an ordinal risk indicator, not a calibrated probability.
  • Odds ratios come from different populations (adolescent idiopathic, neuromuscular, and adult spinal deformity). Apply the sections relevant to your patient; a pediatric AIS case should weight the Hemoglobin (Section 1) and Deformity (Section 4) factors most heavily, an adult deformity case the Comorbidity (Section 2) and Surgical Approach (Section 5) factors.
  • For a validated single-outcome probability, use a published model directly (e.g., Soliman hemoglobin ladder, Gao pediatric score, Durand random-forest ASD model).
  • This worksheet addresses bleeding/transfusion risk only; it does not estimate overall perioperative complication risk.
